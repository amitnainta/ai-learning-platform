Hallucination is when a [large language model](glossary:large-language-model)
confidently generates text that is false or fabricated — an invented
citation, a wrong fact stated as certain, a plausible-sounding but incorrect
answer. It happens because the model has no built-in mechanism for "I don't
know": it always generates the most plausible-sounding continuation
available, whether or not that continuation is true. Fluent, confident
phrasing from a model is not evidence of accuracy, which is why
fact-checking anything that matters is essential.
