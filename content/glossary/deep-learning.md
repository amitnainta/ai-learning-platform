Deep learning is a branch of [machine learning](glossary:machine-learning)
that uses [neural networks](glossary:neural-network) with many stacked
layers ("deep" refers to the number of layers, not to any figurative
depth of understanding). Deep learning is behind most of the recent, highly
publicized advances in AI — image recognition, speech recognition, and
large language models are all built with deep neural networks.
