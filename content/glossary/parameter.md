A parameter (sometimes called a weight) is one of the internal, adjustable
values inside a [machine learning](glossary:machine-learning) model.
Training is the process of nudging these values, often millions or billions
of them, so the model's predictions get closer to correct. A model's
"size" is usually described by its parameter count — a
[large language model](glossary:large-language-model) with "70 billion
parameters" has that many individually-tunable internal values.
