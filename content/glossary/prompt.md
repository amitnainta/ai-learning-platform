A prompt is the text input given to a [large language model](glossary:large-language-model)
to elicit a response — a question, an instruction, or a partial piece of
text for the model to continue. Because the model generates its response
[token](glossary:token) by token based on everything in the prompt, how a
prompt is worded meaningfully affects the output, which is the whole basis
of prompt engineering.
