Return on investment (ROI) measures the value gained from an investment
relative to its cost. For an AI [use case](glossary:use-case), a credible
ROI calculation has to account for the full cost — not just the technology,
but data preparation, integration, change management, and ongoing
maintenance — against the full, realistic benefit, not just the optimistic
projected upside.
