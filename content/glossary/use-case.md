A use case is a specific, concrete application of a technology to a real
problem — not "use AI," but "use a model to flag likely-fraudulent
transactions before they're approved." Evaluating AI initiatives one
concrete use case at a time, rather than as an abstract strategic
direction, makes it possible to reason about data availability, stakes, and
[ROI](glossary:roi) in specific, checkable terms.
