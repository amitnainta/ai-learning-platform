Generative AI refers to AI systems that create new content — text, images,
audio, video, or code — rather than only classifying input into fixed
categories or predicting a number. [Large language models](glossary:large-language-model)
are the most prominent example for text; similar approaches (diffusion
models, for instance) generate images and audio. What all generative
systems share is that their output is newly synthesized, not retrieved
from a lookup table of pre-written responses.
