An algorithm is a well-defined, step-by-step procedure for solving a problem
or performing a computation. In [machine learning](glossary:machine-learning),
"the algorithm" usually refers specifically to the training procedure — the
process that adjusts a model's internal values based on training examples —
as distinct from the model itself, which is the resulting structure that
makes predictions.
