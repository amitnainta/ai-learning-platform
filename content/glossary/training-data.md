Training data is the set of examples a [machine learning](glossary:machine-learning)
model learns from. In [supervised learning](glossary:supervised-learning),
each example is paired with the correct answer (a labeled photo, an email
marked "spam" or "not spam"), and the training process adjusts the model to
make its predictions match those answers as closely as possible. The
quality, quantity, and representativeness of training data has an outsized
effect on how well — and how fairly — a resulting model performs.
