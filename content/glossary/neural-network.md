A neural network is a [machine learning](glossary:machine-learning) model
structure made of layers of simple, connected units ("neurons"), loosely
inspired by (but much simpler than) biological brains. Each connection has
an adjustable [parameter](glossary:parameter) that training tunes. Stacking
many layers together is what's called [deep learning](glossary:deep-learning),
the approach behind most modern AI breakthroughs, including large language
models.
