Supervised learning is a [machine learning](glossary:machine-learning)
approach where every example in the [training data](glossary:training-data)
is paired with the correct answer — a photo labeled "cat," an email marked
"spam." The training process adjusts the model to match those labeled
answers as closely as possible. It's the most common and most intuitive
category of machine learning, though not the only one — some approaches
learn from unlabeled data instead.
