Artificial intelligence (AI) is the broad field of building systems that
perform tasks we'd normally say require human intelligence — recognizing
images, understanding language, making predictions, or making decisions.
It spans many different techniques, from simple rule-based systems to
large-scale [machine learning](glossary:machine-learning). Most of what
people mean by "AI" today refers specifically to machine learning systems,
but the term itself is older and broader than that.
