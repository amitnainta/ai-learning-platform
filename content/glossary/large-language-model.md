A large language model (LLM) is a [machine learning](glossary:machine-learning)
model trained on huge amounts of text, which generates language by
repeatedly predicting the most likely next [token](glossary:token) given
everything that came before it. LLMs power tools like ChatGPT, Claude, and
Gemini. Their scale — often hundreds of billions of
[parameters](glossary:parameter) — is what allows "predict the next word"
to produce output that can write code, hold a conversation, or summarize a
document.
