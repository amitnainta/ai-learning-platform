## Who this is for

You write code, ship software, or work close enough to a codebase that
"how does this actually work" is the question you can't leave unanswered.
Maybe you're a backend engineer whose team just got asked to "add some AI"
to the product. Maybe you're a data analyst who wants to go further than
spreadsheets. Maybe you already use AI coding tools every day and want the
concepts underneath them to click.

This path assumes you're comfortable learning technical material, but it
does **not** assume you already know machine learning. Zero Knowledge really
means zero knowledge of AI/ML specifically.

## What you'll learn

- **Zero Knowledge**: what AI and machine learning actually are, how a model
  differs from a traditional program, and the vocabulary you'll need for
  everything that follows.
- **Intermediate**: how large language models work well enough to build with
  them confidently, and how to write prompts that get reliable results.
- **Advanced**: what it takes to run AI systems in production — deployment,
  monitoring, responsible-AI practices, and the operational side (MLOps)
  that separates a demo from a shipped feature.

## How this path is organized

Each level is a short sequence of courses, and each course is a short
sequence of content items — a mix of original lessons written for this
platform and curated links out to some of the best explanations already on
the internet, each one clearly attributed to its source. Work through a
level in order the first time; after that, jump around as needed.
