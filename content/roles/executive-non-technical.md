## Who this is for

You make decisions that involve AI — whether to invest in it, how to
evaluate a vendor's claims, how to talk about it to your board or your team
— but your job isn't to build the models yourself. You want to understand
AI well enough to ask sharp questions and spot hype, without spending a
year learning to code first.

This path assumes no technical background. It does assume you're comfortable
with business and strategy concepts.

## What you'll learn

- **Zero Knowledge**: what AI actually is (and isn't), demystified without
  jargon, so you can follow any AI conversation in your organization.
- **Intermediate**: how to think about AI strategically — where it creates
  real business value, how to evaluate opportunities, and how to talk about
  responsible AI use with your team.
- **Advanced**: how to lead AI adoption at an organizational level —
  transformation programs, governance, and risk management, informed by how
  other organizations are actually doing this.

## How this path is organized

Each level is a short sequence of courses, and each course is a short
sequence of content items — a mix of original lessons written for this
platform and curated links out to some of the best explanations already on
the internet, each one clearly attributed to its source. Work through a
level in order the first time; after that, jump around as needed.
